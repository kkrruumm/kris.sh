** About
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: about
:END:
Hey! My name is Kris.

I like free (as in freedom) open source software, networking, and cybersecurity.

I upload the occasional video to [[https://www.youtube.com/@krumothyy][my YouTube channel]] that may align with a [[/posts][blog post]] here, both of which are typically related to Linux systems administration or security.

I create and maintain various things on [[https://github.com/kkrruumm][my Github]], typically in shell script.

I occasionally create, correct, and/or maintain documentation and packages for open source projects.

I am an administrator on the [[https://procursus.social][procursus.social]] Mastodon instance, as well on the [[https://justsome.photos][JustSome.Photos]] Pixelfed instance.

I am currently staff @ [[https://iw4x.io/][IW4x]].

Information about public services I host can be found on the [[/services][services page]].

This website was created with [[https://www.gnu.org/savannah-checkouts/gnu/emacs/emacs.html][Emacs]] and is hosted with [[https://caddyserver.com/][Caddy]] on a server running [[https://voidlinux.org/][Void Linux]].
